ABOUT OUR BUSINESS

Where Nature Finds a Home and Every Space Comes to Life

FLORISHINE is a plant shop dedicated to bringing the beauty of nature closer to everyone through real, living plants and gardening essentials.

Our goal is to make plant shopping easy and enjoyable while inspiring everyone to grow plants and create greener spaces.

Our Business Background

FLORISHINE was developed as a startup business concept that aims to make living plants and gardening products more accessible through an online platform. It helps customers discover products while providing sellers with an additional way to showcase their offerings.

Our Mission

To provide quality living plants and reliable gardening essentials while inspiring people to embrace nature, nurture plant life, and create greener, more beautiful surroundings.

Our Vision

To become a trusted and recognized plant shop that brings people closer to nature, inspires greener homes and beautiful spaces, and builds a growing community of individuals who share a passion for plants and sustainable living.

Our Objectives

• To provide convenient online plant shopping.
• To showcase different living plants and gardening products.
• To help customers access product information.
• To support small plant sellers through online visibility.
• To encourage gardening and greener surroundings.

UNDERSTANDING THE NEED

The Problem & Our Solution

Making plant shopping more accessible and convenient through a digital platform.

The Problem

Plant buyers and small plant sellers experience different challenges, such as:

  • Limited access to different plant varieties.
  • Difficulty checking plant prices and availability.
  • Time-consuming visits to physical plant shops.
  • Limited online visibility for small plant sellers.
  • Lack of accessible plant care information.

Our Solution

FLORISHINE offers a convenient online platform where customers can explore plants and gardening products while helping small sellers showcase their products.

  • Browse plants and gardening products.
  • View product photos, prices, and information.
  • Add items to the shopping cart.
  • Place orders and track order status.
  • Manage products, availability, and sales through the seller dashboard.

Our Goal

To connect plant buyers and sellers through a simple and convenient online platform.
